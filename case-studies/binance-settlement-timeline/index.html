Frequently Asked Questions

How much did Binance pay total in 2023?
$4.3 billion global resolution including OFAC ($968M), FinCEN ($3.4B), and CFTC components. CEO CZ also pleaded guilty and resigned.
What agencies were involved in the Binance case?
OFAC (sanctions), FinCEN (AML/BSA), DOJ (criminal), and CFTC (commodities). Coordinated global resolution.
Is Binance under ongoing monitoring?
Yes. Binance is under a 5-year compliance monitor as part of the settlement. The monitor oversees compliance program implementation.
Did the Binance CEO face personal liability?
Yes. CZ pleaded guilty to anti-money laundering violations, resigned as CEO, and paid a $50M personal fine. He was sentenced to 4 months prison in April 2024.
