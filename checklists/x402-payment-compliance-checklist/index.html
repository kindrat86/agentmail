x402 Payment Compliance Checklist

The pre-launch checklist for every x402 payment agent.

How to use

How to use: work through each section before your first x402 payment goes live.

Before the first payment

  • Screen every counterparty wallet against the SDN list
  • Screen the full x402 flow — both sides of the round-trip
  • Verify your rail does not screen for you (it does not)
  • Decide fail-open vs fail-closed (fail-closed is the safe mode)

In the flow

  • Insert the screen between the 402 and the settle
  • Keep latency under 100 ms per check
  • Log: wallet, timestamp, match status, list version

After launch

  • Monitor hourly SDN updates (947 wallets, 19,218 names, 16 jurisdictions)
  • Review blocked attempts weekly
  • Re-run the checklist after any rail or agent change

Why this exists

The 2026 SEI report documents the exposure: payment rails do not screen recipients. This checklist closes the gap in the agent path.
