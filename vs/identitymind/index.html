SanctionsAI vs IdentityMind

An honest comparison of SanctionsAI and IdentityMind for teams wiring compliance into agent payments.

TL;DR

TL;DR: IdentityMind is identity/risk decisioning for onboarding; SanctionsAI is a pre-payment screening API for agents. They are not direct substitutes for most teams: one scores identities at onboarding, the other screens every payment.

What IdentityMind does well

  • identity-based risk scoring
  • Established tooling in its own category

Where it falls short for agent payments

  • Not built for sub-100 ms pre-payment decisions at agent volume
  • Pricing and capabilities for the agent path: verify current docs

Where SanctionsAI wins

  • One HTTP/MCP call before money moves — under 100 ms
  • 947 OFAC wallets, 19,218 names, 16 jurisdictions, hourly sync
  • Free tier (5 checks/day), $19/mo dev plan, MIT-licensed self-host

When to use which

IdentityMind when you need its core category. SanctionsAI when an agent — not a human — is the one paying. They compose.

Methodology

Methodology

Scoring dimensions: screening scope, agent-path support (HTTP/MCP/CLI), latency, pricing model, and self-hosting. Each score level: Low = not documented or limited, Medium = partial, High = strong and documented. Not scored: certifications we cannot verify, private-beta features, and internal policies.
