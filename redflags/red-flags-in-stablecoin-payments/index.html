Red Flags in Stablecoin Payments

The stablecoin payment red flags compliance teams look for.

The list

  • Destination wallet on or adjacent to the sanctions list
  • Mixer-funded inflows preceding a large transfer
  • Fresh wallet with no history receiving funds
  • Structuring - transfers split under thresholds
  • Counterparty jurisdiction in an embargoed region
  • Rapid pass-through through multiple addresses

Why stablecoin flows get flagged

Stablecoins are the default agent rail - fast, cheap, and irreversible. The same properties attract evasion flows; the enforcement record tracks USDT/USDC-linked cases.

The issuer-backstop gap

Issuer blocklists are backstops, not screens - the obligation to check the destination runs to the payer. The stablecoin post covers the distinction.

The control

Screen the destination wallet on every stablecoin payment, flag the patterns above, fail closed, and log with list versions.
