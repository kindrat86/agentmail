Red Flags in Government Contracts

The government-contract red flags compliance teams look for.

The list

  • Vendor owned 50%+ by a designated official or state entity
  • Vendor jurisdiction in a sanctioned region
  • State-linked payment rails
  • Fresh vendor with a large first contract payment
  • Subcontractors with sanctioned-adjacent owners
  • Payments routed through sanctioned intermediaries

Why procurement gets flagged

Government exposure runs through supply chains and state-linked vendors. The Russia program's reach over state entities makes the ownership surface central.

The control

Screen the vendor, its owners, and its jurisdiction before contracting and before each payment; flag the patterns above; fail closed; log every decision.
