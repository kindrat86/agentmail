Red Flags in Peer-to-Peer Transactions

The peer-to-peer transaction red flags compliance teams look for.

The list

  • Fresh wallet with no history receiving funds
  • Structuring - payments split to stay under thresholds
  • Counterparty jurisdiction in an embargoed region
  • Rapid pass-through to a mixer or exchange
  • Mismatch between stated purpose and flow
  • Wallet on or adjacent to the sanctions list

Why P2P gets flagged

P2P flows lack the intermediary controls of exchanges - the counterparty surface is direct. The screen is the compensating control.

The control

Screen the counterparty wallet and jurisdiction on every P2P payment; flag the patterns above; fail closed; log every decision.
