China sanctions risk rating

China is subject to targeted sanctions on specific entities and individuals related to human rights abuses, military-civil fusion, and fentanyl. Not comprehensively sanctioned.

Risk rating: Medium-High · Sanctions type: Targeted

Risk factors

  • Uyghur Human Rights Policy Act designations
  • Military-civil fusion entity list
  • Chinese military companies (NS-CMIC)
  • Fentanyl-related designations
  • Hong Kong autonomy sanctions

Detailed assessment

China is NOT comprehensively sanctioned. Targeted sanctions address specific concerns: human rights abuses in Xinjiang (UFLPA, EO 13959), military-civil fusion (NS-CMIC list), fentanyl trafficking, and Hong Kong autonomy erosion. Most trade with China is permitted. Compliance teams should screen counterparties against the SDN list and NS-CMIC list.
