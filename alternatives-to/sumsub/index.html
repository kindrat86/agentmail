5 Best Sumsub Alternatives in 2026 (Honest Comparison)

Sumsub publishes its pricing openly: $1.35 per verification on the Basic plan ($149 minimum monthly commitment) and $1.85 per verification on the Compliance plan ($299 minimum). The two compose rather than compete: Sumsub (or any KYC vendor) verifies the human at the front door; SanctionsAI gates each payment the agent makes afterwards. Having KYC in place does not screen the wallet address an agent is about to pay.

SEON: digital-footprint fraud scoring before documents

SEON describes itself as an AI fraud prevention and AML compliance platform built on 1,100+ real-time signals, covering customer screening, payment screening, transaction monitoring, and case management. Choose it over Sumsub when the primary job is fraud decisioning before a user has uploaded any documents; Sumsub's centre of gravity is document-centric verification. It is not a sanctions gate for an autonomous payment path.

Persona: modular KYC/KYB you reconfigure, not re-plumb

Persona describes itself as a flexible, secure way to verify real people and real businesses online, with a modular platform covering government ID, Document AI, selfie checks, and database checks across the identity lifecycle. Choose it over Sumsub when your onboarding flow will change shape over time and you want to reconfigure the workflows yourself. Like the others here, it is an onboarding platform, not a payment-path gate.

Veriff: high document coverage across many countries

Veriff calls itself an enterprise identity verification platform supporting 12,500+ documents from 230+ countries and territories in 50 languages, with AML screening against sanctions, PEPs, and adverse media. Strongest substitute when document coverage across geographies is your constraint, for example onboarding users who hold uncommon document types. Onboarding platform, not a payment-path gate.

Shufti: KYC, KYB and KYI with bundled AML screening

Shufti (formerly Shufti Pro) describes itself as a global identity verification platform for KYC and AML, spanning KYC, KYB, KYI for investors, ongoing monitoring, and FRAML. Substitute it when you need business verification bundled with identity verification from a single vendor. Not a payment-path sanctions gate.

Where Sumsub still wins

  • One contract covering KYC, KYB, AML screening, transaction monitoring, fraud prevention, and the Travel Rule
  • Published per-verification pricing with published minimums, which is rarer in this market than it should be
  • Orchestration, case management, and analytics bundled with the verification stack

Where Sumsub falls short, and who picks up each gap

  • Per-verification pricing with $149-299/mo minimums prices out low-volume agent projects (picked up by SanctionsAI's free tier and $19/mo Dev plan)
  • Built for human onboarding flows, not a sub-100 ms autonomous payment decision (picked up by SanctionsAI)
  • Per-verification surcharges ($0.04 phone) and SMS fees tiered by country add moving parts to cost forecasting (picked up by flat Dev/Pro plans and the $0.05/check x402 rate)
  • Identity-first: does not gate the specific wallet address an agent is about to pay (picked up by SanctionsAI's wallet screening)

The honest verdict

There is no single best Sumsub alternative, because Sumsub bundles five jobs into one contract. Price-driven switchers at low volume land on SanctionsAI or SEON-style fraud scoring; flow-control switchers on Persona; geographic document coverage on Veriff or Shufti; and anyone whose real job is "gate the wallet my agent is about to pay" on SanctionsAI.
