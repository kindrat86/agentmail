OFAC Virtual Currency Guidance

OFAC's published guidance on sanctions compliance obligations for the virtual currency industry, including cryptocurrency exchanges, wallet providers, and DeFi protocols.

TL;DR

TL;DR: OFAC has published guidance stating that sanctions obligations apply to the virtual currency industry, including exchanges, wallet providers, and other participants. Digital assets do not change the underlying rules.

What the guidance says

OFAC's position is that the same sanctions obligations that apply to traditional finance apply to virtual currency. Exchanges, wallet providers, and others in the industry must screen for sanctioned parties, block listed assets, and report blocked property just as a bank would. The fact that value moves as a token or an address instead of a wire does not create an exemption.

Why crypto is a focus

Sanctioned parties have used digital assets to move value, and OFAC has designated crypto wallets and the services that support them. That makes wallet screening a practical necessity, not a theoretical concern. OFAC has also applied its 50 Percent Rule and designation authority to crypto actors, so exposure can spread through a network quickly.
