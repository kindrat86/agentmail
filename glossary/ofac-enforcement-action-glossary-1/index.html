OFAC Enforcement Action Glossary Part 1

What an OFAC enforcement action is, and what a penalty actually looks like.

TL;DR

TL;DR: An OFAC enforcement action is a formal penalty or settlement against a party that violated sanctions. Civil penalties start at $356,000 per violation, and intent is not required.

What an enforcement action is

An enforcement action is how OFAC, the Office of Foreign Assets Control, holds a party accountable for a sanctions violation. It typically results in a civil monetary penalty, and OFAC publishes these actions to signal expectations to the industries it regulates.

Why enforcement actions matter

The penalties are severe and apply under strict liability, meaning a violation occurs even without intent or knowledge. Civil penalties start at $356,000 per violation. This makes prevention far cheaper than the cost of an enforcement action after the fact.

How agents avoid enforcement actions
