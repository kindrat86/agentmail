OFAC screening implementation timeline

Start with a free same-day integration, then add scoring, governance, and auditing over the following weeks.

TL;DR

TL;DR: You can screen on day one with a free tier, wire screening into your payment path in week one on a paid plan, and add risk scoring, governance, and auditing within the first month.

Day one

Begin immediately with the free tier: 5 checks per day, no signup, no key. This is enough to test the API and check a handful of counterparties while you plan the full integration. The response is a simple ALLOW or BLOCK in under 100 ms, so there is no setup friction.

Week one

Move to the Dev plan ($19 per month for 10,000 checks) and wire screening into your actual payment path. Add the STAMP step so every decision is logged, and set up dispute_open so a flagged address has a review path. At this point screening runs on every real transaction.

Month one

  • Enable risk_score for a risk view on borderline matches.
  • Add kya_verify to document counterparties.
  • Schedule periodic audits of past decisions.
  • Train staff on what a match means and how to respond.
