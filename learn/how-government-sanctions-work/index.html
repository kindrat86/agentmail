How Government-Official Sanctions Work

The government-tier designation pattern, explained.

Overview

Government officials are often designated en bloc - a head of state, ministers, and senior officials on the same day under one authority. Putin, Lavrov, Mishustin, and Shoigu were designated together on February 25, 2022.

The pattern

  1. OFAC designates the leadership tier under a program authority
  2. State-owned entities follow (banks, defense firms)
  3. The 50% rule extends reach to entities they control

The screening shape

Screen the counterparty name, its owners, and its state-linked status. A payment to a Russian government entity is in scope - the government-entity scenario shows the workflow.

Why it matters

Government entities surface in supply chains, procurement, and state-linked vendors. The screen catches the named tier; ownership screening catches the shells.
