What may be inside the Elliptic quote

Price only becomes comparable after the buyer defines the job. Elliptic presents several capabilities that solve different parts of a crypto-risk workflow. Asking for "Elliptic pricing" without naming the required workflow can produce a quote that is difficult to compare with another vendor.

Lens screening and monitoring

Elliptic describes Lens as a workspace for wallet screening, transaction screening, continuous monitoring, configurable risk rules, alerts, and case handling. A buyer should state whether the need is a point-in-time check, ongoing monitoring, analyst review, or all of those. The quote should identify which capabilities are included and which create separate usage or service charges.

Official source: Elliptic Lens

Investigator case work

Elliptic describes Investigator as a blockchain-forensics product for tracing funds, mapping connected entities, building visual cases, and exporting evidence. That is a different procurement question from inserting a risk check into a payment path. SanctionsAI recommends asking whether Investigator access is bundled with screening, licensed separately, limited by analyst access, or governed by another usage measure.

Official source: Elliptic Investigator

Data Fabric and API delivery

Elliptic presents Data Fabric as blockchain data and intelligence delivered into customer workflows. Its public product copy refers to feeds, files, and API access. Buyers should define whether they need a hosted interface, direct API calls, bulk data delivery, internal-pipeline integration, or a combination. Those are materially different deliverables even when they draw on the same vendor data.

SanctionsAI quote checklist

Send the same written requirements to every vendor. This makes the returned proposals comparable and exposes exclusions before implementation begins.

  • Use case: name the decision being made, such as onboarding a wallet, screening a withdrawal, monitoring a cleared address, or investigating an alert.
  • Delivery: specify hosted workspace, synchronous API, data feed, downloadable file, or internal-system integration.
  • Volume definition: ask what the contract counts as usage, how retries and monitoring events are treated, and what happens when the agreed allowance is exceeded.
  • User access: ask which analyst, investigator, administrator, and audit roles are included.
  • Data scope: list the assets, chains, bridges, entity labels, and risk categories required by your actual workflow rather than buying a broad claim.
  • Decision evidence: request a sample response, explanation fields, audit trail, export options, and retention terms.
  • Implementation: separate vendor subscription charges from setup, integration, training, professional services, and internal engineering work.
  • Operations: ask for support hours, incident communication, service commitments, renewal mechanics, and termination or data-export terms in writing.
  • Trial: define the trial dataset, production restrictions, success criteria, expiration behavior, and whether a trial converts automatically.

How SanctionsAI would evaluate the trial

A trial should test the decision path, not just the interface. Use a controlled set of known benign, sanctioned, ambiguous, and operationally difficult examples that your compliance team is permitted to use. Record whether the result is explainable, whether analysts can reproduce it, how the product handles uncertain exposure, and what evidence is available for review.

Do not infer production cost from trial access. Ask Elliptic to turn the tested workflow into a written commercial proposal that states the unit of usage, included products, implementation scope, support, renewal basis, and all material exclusions. If a line item cannot be mapped to a requirement, ask why it is present.

Elliptic and SanctionsAI are not like-for-like purchases

Elliptic publicly describes a broad blockchain intelligence platform spanning screening, monitoring, investigations, and data delivery. SanctionsAI focuses on a narrower job: keyless sanctions checks that developers and agents can call directly, including name and wallet screening against published sanctions data.

The narrow SanctionsAI scope can be useful when the requirement is a lightweight sanctions signal in an automated path. It does not replace a full blockchain-forensics investigation, continuous transaction-monitoring program, or a regulated institution's broader compliance controls. The correct comparison starts with the required decision and evidence, not with whichever product has the shortest feature list.
