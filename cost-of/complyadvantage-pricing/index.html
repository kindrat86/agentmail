How much does ComplyAdvantage cost?

ComplyAdvantage publishes a self-serve Starter plan from $99/month — unusual in this category. Here is what that price covers, and where it stops.

ComplyAdvantage does publish pricing, which makes it one of the few in this category you can price without a sales call. These figures are read from the vendor's own page, not estimated:

Read from complyadvantage.com/pricing on 2026-07-26. Starter is self-service and includes sanctions and watchlists, PEPs and RCAs, adverse media, customer and company screening, and ongoing monitoring. The $99 figure is the floor of a volume slider, not a flat rate.

What you are buying

ComplyAdvantage is an AML platform selling sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening plus transaction monitoring, with a self-serve entry tier. That framing matters more than the number: the cost of a compliance tool is mostly a function of how much of it you will use.

What moves the ComplyAdvantage number

Cost driverHow it moves the number
Monitored entitiesThe Starter plan is priced by how many entities you keep under ongoing monitoring — 100 to 2,000 — not by how many one-off screens you run.
Essentials vs AgenticThe published Starter price has two variants; the agentic-workflow variant is the more expensive of the two.
Moving to EnterprisePayments screening, transaction monitoring and unlimited usage are Enterprise-only, and Enterprise is quoted rather than published.
Data breadthAdverse media and PEP/RCA data are the expensive part of the dataset; sanctions lists alone are public.

Questions worth asking before you outgrow the published tier

  • What is the annual minimum, and what is the shortest available term?
  • What happens on overage — hard stop, or per-unit billing at a higher rate?
  • Which modules are in the quoted figure and which are add-ons at renewal?
  • Is sanctions screening available without the surrounding platform?
  • What is the contractual latency, and is it measured at the API or in the UI?
  • How many engineering weeks does the vendor expect implementation to take?

Is ComplyAdvantage the right shape for your problem?

ComplyAdvantage is a genuine option for a small team that needs ongoing monitoring of a customer base with PEP and adverse-media coverage, and it is one of the few vendors here you can buy without a sales call. It is still a per-entity monitoring subscription: if you need a stateless list check on a counterparty an agent will never see again, you are paying to monitor a relationship that does not exist.

Is there a free version of ComplyAdvantage?

There is ComplyLaunch, a free programme for early-stage start-ups that you apply to rather than sign up for. The commercial plans start at the published $99/month Starter tier.

Is ComplyAdvantage cheaper than an OFAC screening API?

If you are monitoring a customer base, the first unit fits; if you are screening counterparties an agent meets once, the second does.
