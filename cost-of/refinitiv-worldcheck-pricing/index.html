What you are buying

LSEG World-Check is the risk-intelligence database formerly sold as Refinitiv World-Check: sanctions, PEP, state-ownership and adverse-media records, delivered as a screening product or a data feed. That framing matters more than the number: the cost of a compliance tool is mostly a function of how much of it you will use.

What drives the LSEG World-Check quote

Cost driverHow it moves the number
Data scopeSanctions is the cheap slice. PEP, state-ownership and adverse-media research are what the licence is actually for, and each widens the number.
Delivery modelA hosted screening UI, an API, and a bulk feed you host yourself are three different contracts against the same data.
Named usersScreening-UI access is licensed per named analyst, so cost tracks headcount as well as volume.
Redistribution rightsScreening on behalf of your own customers requires redistribution terms and changes the contract materially.

Questions to put in writing before you sign

  • What is the annual minimum, and what is the shortest available term?
  • What happens on overage — hard stop, or per-unit billing at a higher rate?
  • Which modules are in the quoted figure and which are add-ons at renewal?
  • Is sanctions screening available without the surrounding platform?
  • What is the contractual latency, and is it measured at the API or in the UI?
  • How many engineering weeks does the vendor expect implementation to take?

Is LSEG World-Check the right shape for your problem?

World-Check is bought for research depth — the ownership data behind the OFAC 50% Rule is genuinely hard to reproduce, and that is a real reason to license it. If your obligation is direct SDN list matching before a payment, you are licensing a research library to answer a lookup against a list the US Treasury publishes for free.

Frequently asked questions

How much does World-Check cost?

LSEG does not publish World-Check prices. The product pages carry no figure and offer only “Email sales”, “Call sales” and “Contact sales”. Ranges circulating online, including ones previously published on this site, are estimates of private contracts and not LSEG prices.

Is World-Check the same as Refinitiv World-Check?

It is the same product under new ownership. Refinitiv was acquired by the London Stock Exchange Group, and World-Check is now sold under LSEG Risk Intelligence. Older material, including much of the pricing commentary online, still uses the Refinitiv name.

Do I need World-Check for OFAC compliance?

Not for direct SDN list matching. The OFAC SDN list is published by the US Treasury and free to use — SanctionsAI serves it through an API with a free tier of 5 checks/day, and you are paying for the interface, freshness and audit trail rather than for the list. World-Check earns its licence when you need PEP screening, adverse media, or the ownership research behind the 50% Rule.
