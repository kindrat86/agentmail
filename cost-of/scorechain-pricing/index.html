What you are buying

Scorechain is a crypto compliance platform for wallet risk scoring and transaction monitoring, aimed at European VASPs. That framing matters more than the number: the cost of a compliance tool is mostly a function of how much of it you will use.

Questions to put in writing before you sign

  • What is the annual minimum, and what is the shortest available term?
  • What happens on overage — hard stop, or per-unit billing at a higher rate?
  • Which modules are in the quoted figure and which are add-ons at renewal?
  • Is sanctions screening available without the surrounding platform?
  • What is the contractual latency, and is it measured at the API or in the UI?
  • How many engineering weeks does the vendor expect implementation to take?

Is Scorechain the right shape for your problem?

Scorechain is a reasonable mid-market fit for a VASP with a compliance function. It is still a platform subscription, which is a poor match for a single pre-payment list check embedded in an agent's code path.

Frequently asked questions

Does Scorechain publish pricing?

No. Scorechain quotes based on address volume, chain modules and seats.

Is Scorechain cheaper than Chainalysis or Elliptic?

Scorechain positions itself at the mid-market rather than the enterprise tier, but since none of the three publish list prices, any ranking is a claim about positioning and not a verified price comparison.

Do I need risk scoring, or just sanctions screening?

Sanctions screening is the legal obligation: do not transact with a designated party. Risk scoring is a judgement about proximity to illicit activity. Many teams need the first and adopt the second later.
